# 8-Bit Hero Clash

An exciting 8-bit pixel art side-view fighting game featuring 5 unique heroes, each with their own abilities and playstyles.

## Features

✨ **5 Playable Heroes:**
- **Werebeast** - Fast claw slashes with rage charge
- **Slayer** - Long reach with powerful blue katana surge
- **Fighter** - Fists and energy orbs with balanced stats
- **Web Runner** - Swift spider abilities with web attacks
- **Mechaman** - Heavy armor with ranged arc pulse blasts

🎮 **Gameplay Mechanics:**
- Local 2-player fighting arena
- Three attack types: Light, Heavy, and Special
- Charge meter system for special abilities
- Health bars with real-time damage feedback
- Screen shake effects on successful hits
- Pixel art 8-bit visual style
- Dynamic arena with parallax background

⚡ **Controls:**

**Player 1:**
- `A` / `D` - Move Left / Right
- `W` - Jump
- `J` - Light Attack
- `K` - Heavy Attack
- `L` - Special Ability

**Player 2:**
- `←` / `→` - Move Left / Right
- `↑` - Jump
- `1` - Light Attack
- `2` - Heavy Attack
- `3` - Special Ability

**General:**
- `SPACE` - Start Game (after hero selection)
- `R` - Restart Game

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Select your hero by pressing the corresponding number:
   - **P1:** Press 1-5
   - **P2:** Press Q-O
3. Press `SPACE` to start the match
4. Defeat your opponent by reducing their health to zero
5. Press `R` to restart and play again

## Game Balance

Each hero has unique stats:
- **Health (HP):** Ranges from 110 to 160
- **Speed:** Movement speed varies by character
- **Jump Height:** Different vertical mobility
- **Damage Values:** Light, Heavy, and Special attacks scale differently
- **Special Cost:** Charge required to perform ultimate ability

## Tips

🔥 **Attack Strategy:**
- Light attacks are fast but deal less damage
- Heavy attacks take longer but hit harder
- Special abilities require full charge but deal massive damage
- Build your charge meter by landing hits!

🛡️ **Defense:**
- Move around the arena to avoid attacks
- Use spacing to keep enemies at range or close the gap
- Time your jumps to dodge incoming attacks

⏱️ **Charge Management:**
- Your charge meter builds automatically and on successful hits
- Save charges for critical moments or go aggressive
- Different heroes have different special costs

## Technical Details

- **Engine:** HTML5 Canvas with Vanilla JavaScript
- **Graphics:** Pixel art sprite rendering
- **Physics:** Gravity-based movement with collision detection
- **Performance:** 60 FPS optimized gameplay
- **No Dependencies:** Pure JavaScript - runs in any modern browser
